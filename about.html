ABOUT FANDS

We don’t sell software.
We implement it.

Fands is an AI implementation agency for small and mid-size businesses in the United States. We find where AI will pay off, build it into the way your team already works and stay to make sure it keeps paying off.

Why Fands
exists.

Small businesses were promised that AI would change everything. Most got a stack of subscriptions, a few experiments and the same busy week as before.

The tools are not the problem. They are powerful, and many are cheap. What is missing is the work in between: understanding how a business actually runs, choosing the one process worth changing first, connecting the tools to the systems already in place, testing the awkward cases and making sure people trust the result.

Large companies pay consultants and in-house teams to do that work. Small and mid-size businesses usually get a login and a tutorial. Fands was started to close that gap: to bring the discipline of a proper implementation, with a baseline, a plan and ongoing support, at a scale and a price that fits a growing business.

The name says what we do. We take the friction out of everyday operations and replace it with flow: calls answered, leads followed up, admin done in the background and a business that gets found.

What we
believe.

The views that shape every project we take on, and every one we turn down.

01

AI is a tool, not a strategy.

The goal is never “use AI”. The goal is fewer missed calls, faster replies or hours back in the week. AI is how we get there when it is the right tool.

02

Problem first, pitch second.

We ask what the bottleneck costs before we talk about ourselves. If AI is not the right answer, we say so on the first call.

03

Start small. Prove it. Expand.

One working system that pays for itself beats an ambitious roadmap that never ships. The next build is earned by the last one.

04

If it isn’t measured, it didn’t happen.

Every build starts from a baseline and is judged by one agreed number, reported every month.

05

People stay in control.

AI prepares and drafts. People approve what matters. Every system has an exception path and a manual fallback.

06

No lock-in, no black boxes.

Your accounts, your data, your workflows, documented in plain language. You can always take it in-house.

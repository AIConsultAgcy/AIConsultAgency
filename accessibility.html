Accessibility Statement

We want everyone to be able to use fands.io, whatever device, browser or assistive technology they rely on.

Our standard

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA, and we review accessibility whenever we add pages or features.

What we do

  • Semantic page structure with a single main heading per page, landmarks and a “Skip to content” link.
  • Full keyboard navigation with visible focus indicators, including the menu, tabs and demo.
  • Text alternatives for meaningful images and video, and decorative graphics hidden from screen readers.
  • Respect for the “reduce motion” setting: animations are paused or simplified, and the hero animation can be paused.
  • Layouts that work from small phones to large screens and with browser zoom up to 200%.
  • The UserWay widget, which lets you adjust contrast, text size, spacing and more.

Known limitations

  • Background videos have no audio but do not yet have text descriptions beyond their labels.
  • Third-party tools, such as the Cal.com booking window, are outside our direct control. If one gives you trouble, email us and we will book your call directly.

Report a barrier

If something on the Site is hard to use, tell us at hello@fands.io with the page and what happened. We aim to reply within five business days and to provide the information you need in another format in the meantime.

See also our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

About Fands

Before
we build.

Straight answers to the questions we hear on almost every first call.

How much does the AI Readiness Audit cost?

The first call is free. The AI Readiness Audit is a paid, fixed-fee engagement, quoted on that call once we know how many processes and tools are involved. Every build after it is also quoted at a fixed price before work starts. Third-party software and usage fees are billed separately, at cost.

How long does AI implementation take?

The audit usually takes one to two weeks. Most first builds, such as an AI receptionist, lead follow-up or an invoicing workflow, go live in two to six weeks. Custom AI agents and apps take longer and are scoped separately. You get a timeline with the fixed quote.

Do we need technical experience?

No. We handle the setup, integrations and testing. Your team needs to know the process and be available to review drafts. We train the people who use the system and leave a plain-language runbook behind.

What happens to our data?

Your data stays in your accounts wherever possible, and you own everything we build. We use business tiers of AI providers that do not train on your data, give systems only the access they need, and sign an NDA on request. Sensitive data rules, such as for health or finance, are scoped in the audit.

Will AI make decisions on its own?

Not the important ones. In a first build, AI prepares information and drafts actions. People approve consequential outputs. Low-confidence cases go to a named person, with a manual fallback.

Do we need to replace our tools?

Usually not. We start with what you already use, such as your CRM, calendar, inbox and accounting software, and connect it. We check permissions, API access and data quality before committing to an integration.

What happens after launch?

With the Managed AI Retainer we monitor every system, fix issues, improve what works and review the numbers with you each month. You can also take the runbook and run it yourself.

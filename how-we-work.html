HOW WE WORK · FIVE STEPS

From first call
to monthly review.

We don’t sell software. We implement it. Every engagement follows the same five steps, and every step ends with something you can see, check and decide on, starting with a measured baseline.

Book a Free Call Our services
  1. 01
    Free · 30 minutes

    The first call

    We start with your problem, not our pitch. Where does the work slow down? Where do customers slip away? What does that cost you each month? Only after we understand the pain and its price do we tell you how we would approach it.

    • Your biggest bottleneck, in your words
    • A rough cost of the problem
    • An honest view of whether AI is the right fix

    Strategy session · estimate

    Strategy and a fixed quote

    Build · usually 2–6 weeks

    Build and launch

    • Integrations with your current tools
    • Testing on real edge cases
    • Human approval and fallback paths
    • Team training and a runbook

How we
keep it honest.

The rules we work by on every project, whatever its size.

01

Baseline first

No numbers, no promises. We measure before we build, so the result can be checked.

02

Fixed price

Scope and price are agreed before work starts. Software fees are passed through at cost.

03

People in control

AI drafts and prepares. People approve anything consequential, with a manual fallback.

04

You own it

Accounts, data and workflows live in your name, with documentation to run them.

The challenge

Leads arrived from the website, Google, Facebook and phone calls. The owner answered them in the evening, if at all. By then, many homeowners had already booked a competitor who called back first. Nobody could say how many leads were lost each month.

What the audit found

The baseline showed long and uneven reply times, no consistent follow-up after a quote and no single list of open leads. Old quotes that never closed were a sizable, untouched pipeline.

What we built

  • A CRM set up as the one place every lead lands, from every source.
  • An instant, personal first reply by text and email with two call times to choose from.
  • Missed-call text-back for calls the crew can’t take on site.
  • A follow-up sequence after every quote until the homeowner decides.
  • A reactivation campaign for past quotes and review requests after finished jobs.
  • A one-page pipeline dashboard: leads, quotes, won jobs and value.

How people stay in control

The owner approved every message template. For the first month, first replies were reviewed before sending until the tone was right. Pricing questions always go to a person.

Monthly reviews track reply time, conversion and pipeline value. Typical next steps are an AI receptionist for after-hours calls and local SEO to bring in more of the right leads.

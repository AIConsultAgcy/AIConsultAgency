AI Readiness Audit & Roadmap

A paid, fixed-fee audit that turns “we should use AI” into a ranked plan with numbers attached.

What it is

We map how the work actually moves through your business, measure where time and revenue leak, and check which of your tools and data are ready for automation. You get a short list of quick wins, an ROI estimate for each and a 30/60/90-day roadmap. The audit stands on its own: the plan is yours whether or not you build with us.

What’s included

  • Process mapping
  • Baseline measurement
  • Tool and data check
  • Quick wins
  • ROI estimate
  • 30/60/90-day plan
  • Fixed-price build estimate

What we measure

  • TimeHours per week spent on manual, repeatable work
  • SpeedResponse time to calls, leads and customer requests
  • LeakageMissed calls, unanswered leads and dropped follow-ups

A good fit if

  • You know AI could help but not where to start
  • Your team repeats the same tasks every week
  • You want numbers before you commit to a build
Ongoing

Managed AI Retainer

Monitoring, support and monthly optimization for every system we build, so it keeps working after launch.

What it is

AI systems drift: prices change, tools update, new edge cases appear. With the retainer we watch every workflow and assistant, fix issues before your team notices, improve prompts and rules as we learn, and review the numbers with you every month against the baseline from the audit.

What’s included

  • Monitoring and alerts
  • Support and fixes
  • Monthly optimization
  • Monthly results review
  • Small changes and new rules
  • Vendor and tool updates

What we measure

  • ReliabilityUptime and successful runs per system
  • QualityException rate and cases handed to a person
  • OutcomeThe agreed KPI against the audit baseline

A good fit if

  • You want systems that keep improving, not a one-off install
  • You don’t have an in-house technical team
  • You want a monthly number you can show your partners

About the audit and the retainer.

What do we get at the end of the AI readiness audit? {{i:chevron:19}}

A process map, the baseline numbers, a ranked list of quick wins with an ROI estimate for each, a 30/60/90-day plan and a fixed-price estimate for the first build. We present it in a working session with your team.

Do we have to build with Fands after the audit? {{i:chevron:19}}

No. The roadmap is yours. Most clients continue with us because we already know the process, but you can take the plan to your own team or another vendor.

Is the retainer required? {{i:chevron:19}}

No, but it is how systems keep paying off. Without it, you receive the runbook and documentation and can run the system yourself.

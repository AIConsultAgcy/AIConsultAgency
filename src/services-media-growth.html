About content and AI search.

What is GEO and AEO? {{i:chevron:19}}

Generative engine optimization (GEO) and answer engine optimization (AEO) are the practice of making your business easy for AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews to find, trust and cite when someone asks for a recommendation.

Can you guarantee we appear in ChatGPT answers? {{i:chevron:19}}

No one can honestly guarantee that. We improve the signals AI systems rely on and track how often your business is mentioned each month, so you can see the trend.

Will AI-written content hurt our brand? {{i:chevron:19}}

Not when it is done properly. Every piece follows your brand voice guide and is reviewed by a person before it is published.

How much of the content is written by AI? {{i:chevron:19}}

AI drafts and produces the volume. We start with a brand voice guide, a person reviews every piece and you approve the monthly calendar. The goal is content that sounds like you, published consistently.

How soon will we see results from GEO and local SEO? {{i:chevron:19}}

Local profile and listing fixes can show up within weeks. AI citations and organic growth build over several months, and we track them monthly so you see the trend instead of relying on promises.

How do you measure whether this is working? {{i:chevron:19}}

Against numbers we agree at the start: pieces published, engagement, inquiries attributed to content, AI mentions of your business, local rank, profile actions such as calls and directions, and cost per lead for paid media.

Do you need access to our social and ad accounts? {{i:chevron:19}}

We need the access required to publish and manage, granted through each platform’s own sharing settings so the accounts stay in your name. You can remove that access at any time.

AI Receptionist & Inbound Lead Capture

Calls, texts and chats answered 24/7, with appointments booked straight into your calendar.

What it is

An AI voice agent answers your business line in a natural voice, any hour of the day. It answers common questions from your approved information, qualifies the caller, books or reschedules appointments and logs everything in your CRM. The same assistant works over SMS and web chat. Anything outside its rules goes to a named person with the full transcript.

What’s included

  • 24/7 AI voice agent
  • SMS and web chat
  • Appointment booking
  • CRM integration
  • Handoff to a human
  • Call transcripts and summaries

What we measure

  • Answer rateCalls answered, including after hours
  • BookingsAppointments booked by the assistant
  • HandoffsCalls passed to a person, and why

A good fit if

  • Calls go to voicemail when you are busy or closed
  • Your front desk spends hours on routine questions
  • Each missed call is a missed job or appointment

About AI receptionists and agents.

Will callers know they are talking to AI? {{i:chevron:19}}

We recommend the assistant says so at the start of the call. Customers care more about getting a fast, correct answer than about who gives it, and honesty builds trust.

Can we keep our current phone number? {{i:chevron:19}}

Yes. Calls can be forwarded to the AI receptionist always, after hours only or when your line is busy. Your number does not change.

What happens when the AI can’t help? {{i:chevron:19}}

It hands the conversation to a person you choose, by live transfer, text or a callback task, together with a summary and the full transcript.

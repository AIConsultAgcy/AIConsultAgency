Privacy, in plain language.

What we collect through this site, why, and how to reach us about it.

What we collect

The workflow form collects your name, company, work email, where you’d like to start, the handoff you describe, and your consent. The Field Notes form collects your email and consent. We collect nothing else through these forms.

How it is used

We use inquiry details only to reply to you and to prepare for a first conversation. Submitting an inquiry does not sign you up for Field Notes; that is a separate opt-in. We do not sell or rent your details, and we do not send form contents to an AI model.

How it is delivered

Form submissions are delivered to our inbox at hello@fands.io through a form-delivery service. If that service is unavailable, the site opens your own email app with the details filled in, and nothing is sent until you press send.

Analytics

We use privacy-friendly, aggregate page analytics from our hosting provider. It does not use cookies or build a profile of you. No advertising pixels are installed.

Retention and your choices

We keep inquiry emails for as long as they are useful to the conversation and delete them on request. To see, correct, or delete what you sent us, or to leave Field Notes, write to hello@fands.io.

Please don’t send sensitive personal information, credentials, or client confidential information through this site.

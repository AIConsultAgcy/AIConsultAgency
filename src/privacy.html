1. Who we are

Fands (“Fands”, “we”, “us”) is an AI implementation agency based in West Hollywood, California, serving small and mid-size businesses in the United States. This policy covers personal information we collect through fands.io (the “Site”), through our forms, booking tools and email, and when we communicate with prospects and clients. It does not cover the data our clients ask us to process inside their own systems; that is described in our AI & Client Data Policy and governed by our client agreements.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Inquiry form: your name, company, work email, where you would like to start, the description of the process you share and your consent.
  • Field Notes sign-up: your email address and consent.
  • Booking a call: the name, email and any notes you enter when you book through our scheduling provider, Cal.com.
  • Email and calls: anything you choose to send us or tell us during a conversation.

Information collected automatically

  • Usage and device data: pages viewed, links and buttons clicked, referring page, approximate location derived from IP address, browser and device type, collected through Google Analytics 4 (via Google Tag Manager) and Vercel Web Analytics.
  • Technical data: IP address and request logs kept by our hosting provider, Vercel, to deliver and secure the Site.
  • Accessibility preferences: settings you choose in the UserWay accessibility widget, stored in your browser.

We do not collect sensitive personal information through the Site and ask you not to send it, such as health information, government ID numbers, financial account details or credentials.

3. How we use information

  • To reply to your inquiry, schedule and prepare for calls, and provide proposals and services.
  • To send Field Notes, only if you subscribed. Every email has an unsubscribe link.
  • To understand how the Site is used and improve its content and performance.
  • To keep the Site and our systems secure, prevent spam and abuse, and comply with law.

We do not sell personal information for money. We do not use personal information from the Site to train AI models, and we do not send form submissions to an AI model.

5. Cookies and analytics

We use a small number of cookies and similar technologies for analytics and functionality. On your first visit a banner lets you accept or decline analytics cookies. We do not use advertising cookies or advertising pixels. Details and controls are in our Cookie Policy. You can turn off analytics at any time on Your Privacy Choices, and we treat a Global Privacy Control signal from your browser as a request to do the same.

Do Not Track. Browsers’ “Do Not Track” setting has no common standard, so we do not respond to it. We do honor Global Privacy Control, as described above.

6. Client data in our services

When a client engages us, we may access customer, operational or business data in the client’s systems to build and run AI systems. We act as a service provider to that client, process that data only on the client’s instructions and under our agreement with them, and do not use it for our own purposes. Read more in our AI & Client Data Policy.

7. Retention

We keep inquiry and booking information for as long as it is useful for the conversation and any resulting engagement, and generally no longer than 24 months after our last contact, unless we are working together or the law requires longer. Newsletter data is kept until you unsubscribe. Analytics data is kept for the retention period configured in Google Analytics, up to 14 months.

8. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards, including encrypted connections, access limited to the people who need it and two-factor authentication on our accounts. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. California privacy rights

This section applies to California residents and supplements the rest of this policy, including for purposes of the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (“CCPA”). We extend these rights to all California residents whether or not the CCPA applies to us.

Notice at collection

In the last 12 months we have collected the following categories of personal information, from you directly and from your device: identifiers (name, email, IP address); commercial and professional information (company, the process you describe, services you are interested in); internet activity (Site usage); and approximate geolocation. We use them for the purposes in section 3 and keep them for the periods in section 7. We do not collect sensitive personal information for the purpose of inferring characteristics about you.

Selling and sharing

We do not sell personal information for money. Our use of Google Analytics may be considered “sharing” under the CCPA. You can opt out on Your Privacy Choices or with a Global Privacy Control signal. We do not knowingly sell or share the personal information of anyone under 16.

Your rights

  • Know and access the personal information we have collected about you and how we use and disclose it.
  • Delete personal information we collected from you, subject to legal exceptions.
  • Correct inaccurate personal information.
  • Opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information.
  • Limit the use of sensitive personal information, although we do not use it for purposes that require this right.
  • Non-discrimination: we will not treat you differently for exercising your rights.

How to exercise your rights

Fastest: use the data request form to ask for access, correction or deletion. Or email hello@fands.io with the subject “Privacy request”. We will confirm receipt within 10 business days and respond within 45 days, which may be extended once by another 45 days if necessary. We will verify your request by matching information you provide with what we hold. An authorized agent may submit a request on your behalf with your signed permission.

Shine the Light

California Civil Code § 1798.83 lets California residents ask about disclosures of personal information to third parties for their direct marketing. We do not make such disclosures.

10. Other US state privacy rights

Residents of states with comprehensive privacy laws, including Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, Utah, Texas, Oregon and others, may have similar rights to access, correct, delete and obtain a copy of their data and to opt out of targeted advertising, sale or profiling. We honor these requests from residents of any US state using the process above. If we deny a request, you may appeal by replying to our decision, and we will respond within the time required by your state’s law.

11. Children

The Site and our services are for businesses and are not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Changes and contact

We may update this policy as our services or the law change. We will post the new version here with a new “last updated” date and, for material changes, give notice on the Site or by email.

Questions or requests: hello@fands.io, Fands, West Hollywood, California, United States.

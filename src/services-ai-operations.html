Business Process Automation

Repeat work runs in the background, across the tools you use, with a person approving what matters.

What it is

We automate the recurring work that eats your week: creating and chasing invoices, onboarding new clients, reading documents, building reports, scheduling and sorting the inbox. AI reads and drafts; clear rules route the work; your team reviews the exceptions. This includes the integration layer that connects your systems, built on n8n, Make or Zapier together with large language models.

What’s included

  • Invoicing
  • Client onboarding
  • Document processing
  • Reporting
  • Scheduling
  • Email triage
  • n8n, Make, Zapier + LLMs
  • Human approval steps

What we measure

  • Handling timeMinutes of hands-on work per task
  • Cycle timeTime from trigger to finished task
  • Exception rateCases that need a person, and why

A good fit if

  • The same task happens dozens of times a week
  • Data is copied between two or more tools by hand
  • Mistakes or delays cost you money or clients

Lead-to-Revenue Automation

Every lead gets a fast reply, a place in the pipeline and a follow-up that actually happens.

What it is

Most small businesses don’t have a lead problem; they have a follow-up problem. We set up or clean up your CRM, reply to new inquiries within minutes, text back missed calls, ask happy customers for reviews and wake up old leads with timely offers. A simple dashboard shows the pipeline and what it is worth.

What’s included

  • CRM setup
  • Speed-to-lead follow-up
  • Missed-call text-back
  • Review requests
  • Old-lead reactivation
  • Sales pipeline
  • Revenue dashboard

What we measure

  • Speed to leadMinutes from inquiry to first reply
  • Follow-upShare of leads with a scheduled next step
  • ConversionLeads to booked jobs or appointments

A good fit if

  • Leads wait hours or days for a reply
  • You can’t say how many leads you lost last month
  • Past customers rarely hear from you again

Internal Knowledge Assistant & Analytics

Ask your company’s documents a question. See your numbers on one page.

What it is

A private AI assistant that answers your team’s questions from your own documents, such as SOPs, contracts, price lists and policies, and shows where the answer came from. Built with retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), it only uses the sources you approve. We pair it with KPI dashboards that pull your key numbers into one view.

What’s included

  • RAG assistant over company documents
  • Source citations
  • Access by role
  • KPI dashboards
  • Data source connections

What we measure

  • Answer timeTime to find an answer, before and after
  • AccuracyAnswers checked against the source
  • AdoptionWeekly questions asked by the team

A good fit if

  • New hires ask the same questions every week
  • Knowledge lives in a few people’s heads
  • Your numbers are spread across several tools

About automating your business.

Which tools can you automate? {{i:chevron:19}}

Most tools with an API or an integration, including popular CRMs, Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, QuickBooks, calendars, form builders and e-commerce platforms. We confirm access during the audit before quoting a build.

n8n, Make or Zapier: which do you use? {{i:chevron:19}}

Whichever fits your volume, budget and data rules. Zapier and Make are quick to start; n8n suits higher volume or self-hosting. We explain the trade-off and the running cost before we build.

What if the automation gets something wrong? {{i:chevron:19}}

Every workflow has an exception path. Low-confidence or unusual cases go to a named person, and consequential actions such as payments or client emails wait for approval.

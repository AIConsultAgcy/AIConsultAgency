A few things worth knowing.

Do we need to replace our tools? {{i:chevron:19}}

Usually, we start with what you already use. We validate permissions, API access, vendor limits, and data quality before committing to an integration.

How do you measure the outcome? {{i:chevron:19}}

We agree a baseline period, metric owner, and acceptance threshold. We track handling time, cycle time, exception rate, and quality. Reclaimed capacity is reported separately from cash savings.

Will AI make decisions on its own? {{i:chevron:19}}

In our initial workflows, AI prepares information and drafts actions. People approve consequential outputs. Low-confidence cases go to a named person, with a manual fallback.

What happens after the pilot? {{i:chevron:19}}

You receive documentation, training, and an outcome review. We decide whether to expand, maintain, revise, or stop. Ongoing care is a separate engagement.

EXAMPLE ENGAGEMENT · AI OPERATIONS

An invoice inbox that
sorts itself before review.

A small accounting firm processed hundreds of client invoices a month by hand. Business process automation now reads, matches and drafts each one, and a person approves before anything posts.

Industry
Accounting, 12 people
Services
Business Process Automation
Timeline
Audit 2 weeks · build 5 weeks
Primary metric
Handling time per invoice

The challenge

Invoices arrived by email as PDFs in every format. Staff opened each one, typed the vendor, amount, due date and reference into the accounting software and checked it against the purchase order. Month-end meant overtime, and typing errors slipped through.

What the audit found

Most invoices came from a stable set of known vendors, and a majority had a purchase order to match against. The accounting software had a usable API. Handling time per invoice and the error rate were measured over a full month to cover the month-end peak.

What we built

  • A shared inbox watcher that picks up every invoice attachment.
  • AI extraction of vendor, amounts, dates and references, with a confidence score for each field.
  • Rules that match the vendor and purchase order and flag any difference.
  • Draft bill entries created in the accounting software, never posted automatically.
  • A review queue where staff approve, correct or reject in one click.
  • An integration layer built on n8n with an LLM, documented in a runbook.

How people stay in control

Nothing posts without approval. Low-confidence fields are highlighted, mismatches go to a named person, and instructions inside an invoice are treated as text, never as commands.

Monthly reviews track handling time, exception rate and accuracy. Typical next builds are client onboarding automation and an internal knowledge assistant over the firm’s procedures.

This case is built from a typical accounting firm scenario to show our process. Baselines and targets are illustrative and are set for each client during the audit.

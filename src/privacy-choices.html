Your Privacy Choices

Control how fands.io uses analytics in your browser, and exercise your right to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information under California and other state laws.

Analytics and sharing

We do not sell personal information for money or use advertising cookies. We use Google Analytics to understand how the Site is used, which may count as “sharing” under the CCPA. Switch it off below.

Allow analytics on this browser

Analytics is on.

Your choice is saved in this browser and applies right away. If you clear your browser data or use another browser or device, set it again there.

Global Privacy Control

If your browser or an extension sends a Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal, we treat it as a valid request to opt out of sale and sharing and switch analytics off automatically, without you needing to do anything here.

Request access, correction or deletion

Use this form to see the personal information we hold about you, correct it or have it deleted. We confirm within 10 business days and respond within 45 days. To protect your data, we may email you to verify your identity before acting. You can also write to hello@fands.io with the subject “Privacy request”.

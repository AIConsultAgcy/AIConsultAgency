AI & Client Data Policy

How we treat your business data and use AI when we build and run systems for you. Our signed agreement with each client sets the binding terms; this page explains our standard practice in plain language.

1. Our principles

  • Your data, your systems. Data stays in your accounts wherever possible, and you own what we build for you.
  • Least access. Systems and people get only the access a task needs.
  • People in control. AI prepares and drafts; people approve consequential actions.
  • No surprises. We tell you which tools and providers touch your data before we use them.

2. Our role with your data

When we work with your customer, employee or business data, we act as your service provider (a “processor” or “service provider” under privacy laws such as the CCPA). We use your data only to deliver the services you have engaged us for, follow your documented instructions, and do not sell it, share it for advertising or combine it with other clients’ data. A data processing addendum is available on request.

3. AI providers and model training

We build with established AI and automation providers, such as OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, n8n, Make and Zapier, using business or API offerings whose terms state that customer inputs are not used to train their models by default. Where possible, accounts are created in your name so you keep control and billing visibility. We list the providers used in each project in its documentation and tell you before adding a new one.

4. Access and security

  • Access is granted through your accounts and revoked when no longer needed.
  • Credentials are kept in a password manager, never in documents or chat.
  • Our accounts use two-factor authentication.
  • We work on copies or test data where we can, and keep production data out of prompts and logs unless the task requires it.
  • We sign an NDA on request before any data is shared.

5. Human oversight

AI systems we build include an exception path. Low-confidence results go to a named person, and consequential actions, such as payments, pricing, legal or medical statements, and messages that commit your business, require human approval unless you decide otherwise in writing. We test systems against difficult and unusual cases before launch and monitor them afterwards.

6. Sensitive and regulated data

Health, financial, children’s and other regulated data need extra care. We identify it during the AI readiness audit and agree how it will be handled before building. If your business is subject to rules such as HIPAA or GLBA, we only process regulated data with providers and agreements that meet those requirements, and may recommend keeping such data out of AI systems entirely.

7. When AI talks to your customers

AI receptionists, chat and messaging assistants we deploy are designed to identify themselves as automated when asked and, by default, at the start of a conversation. Text messaging follows consent rules under the Telephone Consumer Protection Act and carrier requirements, and calls may be recorded only with the disclosures required by California and other state laws. You remain responsible for your customer-facing policies; we help you set them up.

8. AI-generated content

Content we produce with AI follows your brand voice guide and is reviewed by a person before publishing. We check facts that can be checked, avoid using third-party copyrighted material without permission and label AI-generated media where a platform or law requires it.

9. End of an engagement

When an engagement ends, we hand over documentation and access, remove our access to your systems, and delete or return copies of your data we hold within 30 days unless the law or our agreement requires otherwise.

10. Incidents

If we become aware of unauthorized access to your data in our care, we will notify you without undue delay, share what we know and help you meet any notification duties you have.

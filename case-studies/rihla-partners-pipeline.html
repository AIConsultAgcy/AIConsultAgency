THE STARTING POINT

The right moment,
every week

A hotel that just reopened or an airline that just launched a route needs content now. That is the best moment to pitch a creator partnership, and it passes in weeks. Spotting those moments by hand means reading trade news every day, then hunting for a marketing contact that is rarely published.

On the other side, inbound briefs arrived as plain form emails, with no way to tell a large budget from a small one or to answer the sender while they waited.

What we
built

Outbound that finds its own leads, and inbound that sorts itself.

01

A trigger radar

Every week an agent scans for openings, renovations, route launches and campaigns, and keeps only events from the last 30 days with a public source.

02

Scoring

Each prospect gets a score out of ten with the reason written down, so the strongest fits rise to the top.

03

Contact finding

A second pass checks the event and looks for the right marketing or press contact on the brand's own pages.

04

Drafted first notes

Outreach is drafted around the event itself and saved as a draft. Nothing is sent without approval.

05

A pipeline ledger

One running record of every thread: status, date, contact, event, score and next step, with repeats suppressed.

06

Self-routing briefs

Inbound briefs reach the inbox with every field laid out, larger budgets are flagged PRIORITY, and the sender gets an instant reply with the most relevant case study.

HOW AI IS USED

Agents find the moment. A person sends the note.

The agents do the reading, scoring, contact hunting and first drafts. The founder reviews a short list and approves what goes out. Every prospect carries the source of its trigger event, so nothing rests on a guess.

  • Weekly scans for openings, launches and campaigns
  • A score and a written reason for every prospect
  • Drafts only: a person approves every send

THE STARTING POINT

From referrals to
a system

RSVP Agency is an experiential agency that creates brand experiences, corporate events, weddings and private celebrations across Southern California. It grew through word of mouth and local promotion.

To keep growing, a five-person team needs a website that works as a sales channel, a clear view of every lead and its next step, and a fast way to show a client what an event could look like before it is booked.

What we
built

A public website, a CRM and an internal portal that work as one system.

01

An optimized website

A fast, search-optimized site at thersvpagency.com that shows the agency’s events and turns visitors into inquiries.

02

HubSpot CRM

A configured CRM where every lead has an owner, a stage and a next step.

03

Sales pipeline and connectors

A defined pipeline, plus connectors between the website, HubSpot and the team’s everyday tools, so inquiries arrive without retyping.

04

An internal portal

A portal for the team, built around how an event agency works day to day.

05

AI mockups and examples

AI generation of mockups and examples inside the portal, so a concept reaches the client far faster than building each visual by hand.

HOW AI IS USED

AI where it saves creative time

AI drafts event mockups and examples from a short brief. The team picks, edits and presents what is worth showing, so a concept can be in front of the client early in the conversation.

  • Mockups and examples generated from a short brief
  • The team chooses and edits what the client sees
  • Built into the internal portal, not a separate tool

the agency

A website that brings inquiries, a pipeline that keeps them moving and a portal that speeds up the pitch.

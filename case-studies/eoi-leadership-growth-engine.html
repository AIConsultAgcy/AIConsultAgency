THE STARTING POINT

Good work, no proof
on the page

EOI wins clients in the room. Online, the site had no testimonials, no way to capture a visitor who was not ready to book, and a bare calendar anyone could fill. An audit scored its visibility to AI assistants at 47 out of 100, and there was no tracking to show what any visitor did.

Buyers of coaching now ask ChatGPT and Google who to hire, then check for proof. EOI needed to be findable, believable and measurable.

What we
built

Proof, visibility, measurement and capture, inside the site the firm already had.

01

Client voices

Three short participant videos, captioned, in a custom section on the homepage. Each play is tracked from start to finish.

02

AI search visibility

An llms.txt file, structured data for the firm, its founder and four services, and rewritten titles and descriptions so assistants can describe EOI correctly.

03

Full tracking

Google Analytics 4, a Google Tag Manager container and a Meta pixel, owned by the firm, so every visit, play and form has a number.

04

A lead magnet

Before the Offsite, a guide for leaders planning a team retreat, delivered as a PDF in exchange for a name, email and role.

05

Qualified booking

A five-question form now sits in front of the calendar, so every call arrives with context.

HOW AI IS USED

Visible to the assistants buyers ask

We treated AI assistants as a new front door. The firm, its founder and its services are described in machine-readable form, the site answers the questions buyers actually ask, and the baseline score is tracked so progress is measured, not assumed.

  • llms.txt and schema for the firm, founder and services
  • Titles and descriptions written for how buyers ask
  • A 47/100 baseline tracked every month

THE STARTING POINT

A focused app for
a daily practice

The goal was a simple companion for daily dhikr that stays out of the way: a counter that works with one tap, the qibla direction and prayer times in the same place, and progress that belongs to the user.

We took it from idea to a published product, handling design, development and the App Store release through our own developer profile.

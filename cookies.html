Cookie Policy

A short list of the cookies and similar technologies on fands.io, what each one does and how to switch them off. We use no advertising cookies.

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files a website stores in your browser. Similar technologies, such as local storage and pixels, work in comparable ways. In this policy we call them all “cookies”.

The cookies we use

CategoryProviderWhat it does
Strictly necessaryVercelDelivers the Site securely. Cannot be switched off.
Strictly necessaryFandsRemembers in your browser that you have seen the intro animation and your cookie choice.
FunctionalUserWayStores the accessibility settings you choose in the widget.
FunctionalCal.comRuns the booking window when you schedule a call.
FunctionalYouTube (privacy-enhanced mode)Plays our company film. Nothing loads from YouTube until you press play.
AnalyticsGoogle Analytics 4, via Google Tag ManagerCounts visits, pages and clicks so we can improve the Site. Cookies such as _ga, kept up to 14 months.
Analytics (cookieless)Vercel Web AnalyticsAggregate page views without cookies or cross-site tracking.

We do not use advertising, retargeting or social media tracking cookies, and we set Google’s advertising consent signals to “denied” on every page.

How to control cookies

  • Cookie banner: on your first visit, choose Accept or Decline. Declining switches analytics cookies off.
  • On this Site, any time: change your choice on Your Privacy Choices. Your choice is saved in your browser.
  • Global Privacy Control: if your browser or extension sends a GPC signal, we switch analytics off automatically.
  • Your browser: you can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking strictly necessary cookies may affect how the Site works.
  • Google: you can also install the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Changes

If we add or change cookies, we will update this page and its date. Questions: hello@fands.io. See also our Privacy Policy.
